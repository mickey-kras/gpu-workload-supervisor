vLLM's upstream README directs logo users to its official
[media kit](https://github.com/vllm-project/media-kit/blob/79b2ea5db25b723f2d81dae203a87c5b9a076722/README.md),
which provides official assets for logo use and recommends compact logos for
small spaces, preserving their proportions and clear space. The unmodified
transparent PNG follows that guidance. Its permission basis is the media kit's
published usage guidelines, not the Apache license of the separate vLLM software
repository.

The ComfyUI entry uses the user-supplied transparent yellow/green C illustration
from `ChatGPT Image Oct 9, 2026, 12_05_12 AM(2).png`, copied byte-for-byte. It replaces
the connected-node placeholder. Its upstream origin and official status have not
been verified; it is illustrative artwork, not an official branding claim. Comfy
and ComfyUI are trademarks of Comfy Org.
